  • The sale, auction, licensing, trading, gifting, bartering, or otherwise transferring of all data held on or within your name, including but not limited to:

    4. Usage data regarding ToKa Fitness services, including digital services, physical locations, websites, applications, and specific information about how long you spent looking at our pricing page.
    5. Your IP address, approximate location, device information, browser information, operating system, screen resolution and battery percentage.
    6. Your favourite exercises, including exercises you have never performed but which we have decided you probably enjoy.

    8. Your name, email address, telephone number, billing information, and whatever else you typed into a textbox.
    9. Your interactions with ToKa employees, contractors, robots, vending machines, turnstiles, websites, and other vaguely fitness-related objects.

Location Data

If location services are enabled, ToKa Fitness may collect information regarding your location.

This may include:

  • Your current location.

ToKa Fitness may use this information to improve services and provide advertisements.

This may include injuries, medical conditions, exercise limitations, heart-rate information, nutritional information, training history, and other sensitive information.

We may combine this information with other information about you until the distinction between the datasets becomes largely philosophical.

Artificial Intelligence

Our systems may attempt to determine:
