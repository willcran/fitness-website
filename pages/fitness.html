Fitness Advice

A few tips that can help you to most efficiently keep fit

Consistency

Exercise regularly rather than relying on occasional intense workouts.

Building a consistent routine makes it easier to improve your fitness and maintain healthy habits over time.

Progression

Gradually make your workouts more challenging as your fitness improves.

Increasing repetitions, resistance, or exercise difficulty helps your body continue adapting and getting stronger.

Recovery

Give your body enough time to rest and recover after challenging workouts.

Getting sufficient sleep, eating well, and taking rest days can help prevent excessive fatigue and support your progress.
