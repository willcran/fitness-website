Workouts

We have a wide range of workouts available to our paying customers, a select few are availabe below:

Cycling

Cycling is a great way to stay active while exploring your surroundings.

It can also be an efficient and environmentally friendly way to travel.

Running

Running is an effective way to build stamina and improve cardiovascular fitness.

Whether done outdoors or on a treadmill, it can be easily adapted to different fitness levels.

Swimming

Swimming is a low-impact activity that works many different muscle groups at once.

It can help improve endurance and is a useful skill for both exercise and safety.

Badminton

Badminton is a fast-paced sport that requires quick reactions and good coordination.

Players use a racket to hit a shuttlecock over a net, making it both challenging and enjoyable.

Weight Lifting

Weight lifting involves using resistance, such as dumbbells, to strengthen and develop muscles.

Regular training can also improve overall strength, coordination, and physical fitness.

Tennis

A game of tennis involves hitting a ball back and forth across a net using a racket.

It develops agility, coordination, and endurance while also requiring players to think strategically.
